## Remaining source uncertainty

- **Faint side access needs confirmation.** PDF note 37 points to the faint mark beside the accommodation stair landing. It is drawn as an outward-opening side door with clear wall returns; the original sketch does not establish this entrance conclusively.
- **Changes in height remain schematic.** The short workshop/fuel flights now share a landing as reviewed. Their exact elevations and which levels each flight serves are not measured by the sketches.
- **Written accommodation count remains unresolved.** The sketch establishes ten bedrooms. The written specification describes twenty plus additional upper amenities; those unlocated spaces have not been invented.
- **Service identities and storage access.** Boiler/furnace and tank labels follow the sketch. The storage entrance is conventionally placed from the adjoining passage; exact service partitions and tank identities remain unverified.
- **Dimensions remain schematic.** The helipad retains the marked 43:35 proportion. Other room lengths, stair elevations, hull form and physical door/fixture sizes have not been surveyed.
